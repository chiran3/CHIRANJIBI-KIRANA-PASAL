Who we are

A shop the village trusts.

Chiranjibi Kirana Pasal has been serving the families of Pipal Danda since 2061 BS. Run by a local Rai family. It is not a supermarket. It is the shop your grandmother sent you to as a child.

Located at Hatuwagadhi-6, Bhojpur, we serve the daily needs of our community with honesty, reliability, and prices that make sense. Open every morning, rain or shine.

We stock everything your household needs — rice, lentils, oil, flour, salt, sugar, spices, tea, soap, and daily staples. Seasonal vegetables and fruits fresh from local farms: tomatoes, potatoes, onions, spinach, cauliflower, bananas, and whatever the season brings. Whatever the village needs, we keep it stocked.

A Rai family shop, for the whole village. Still here, still open, still yours.
